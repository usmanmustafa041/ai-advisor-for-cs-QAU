# BSCS Final Year Project Submission Guidelines

This departmental knowledge record consolidates the FYP submission process maintained by the QAU CS academic advisor.

## Report preparation and formatting

- Students must format the final project report according to the QAU Computer Science department guidelines.
- Use of the official Quaid-i-Azam University LaTeX/Overleaf thesis template is highly recommended for standard compliance.

## Supervisor evaluation and clearance

- The complete draft must be reviewed by the assigned project supervisor.
- The supervisor must run a Turnitin plagiarism check for compliance with the applicable HEC similarity limit.
- The supervisor must approve and sign the clearance sheet before the public evaluation is scheduled.

## Public seminar and demonstration

- The FYP defense includes a public seminar presentation and a live demonstration of the working software or application.
- The evaluation panel consists of the external examiner, internal project supervisor, and Chairperson of the Computer Science department.

## Final hand-in

- After passing the defense, students must incorporate the required revisions and prepare the official hardbound copies.
- Hardbound copies must include original approval signatures from the external examiner, supervisor, and Chairperson.
- The approved bound copies and the project's digital source code and assets must be deposited with the CS departmental office.

Current deadlines, copy counts, template versions, and the applicable HEC similarity threshold must be confirmed from the latest departmental announcement because they may change by term.
