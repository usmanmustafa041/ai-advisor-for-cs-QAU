          Abstract                                                                  
                                                                                    
          The main task of the project AI-Powered Chat-Based Academic Advisor to the Computer
          Science Department at Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU) is to design a virtual academic advisor
          capable of properly addressing student inquiries 24 hours a day, without being restricted by office
                                                                                    
          hours and human assistance.                                               
          The system affect the AI and NLP strength to comprehend and answer student inquiries in dialogic
                                                                                    
          and human-like way. It employs a streamlined and multi-lingual BERT (Bidirectional Encoder
          Representations of Transformers) model, which can interpret and process queries in both
          English and Urdu, and maintain entire inclusiveness and convenience to every department student.
                                                                                    
          The chatbot will be able to provide responses to a broad variety of academic questions, such as,
          but not limited to:                                                       
                                                                                    
            •  Registration of courses and conditions of eligibility.               
            •  Distribution of credit hours per semester.                           
                                                                                    
            •  Prerequisites and sequences of academic progression.                 
            •  Terms of payment and fees.                                           
            •  Examination schedules and classes.                                   
            •  Thesis and research project guidelines.                              
                                                                                    
          The assistant also needs to use structured knowledge base to retrieve information that will be
          accurate and reliable to the responses and that will be built with official departmental documents
                                                                                    
          such as regulations, syllabi, academic calendars and policy manuals. The system is designed to be
          dynamically updated and it can easily integrate new policies or changes in the curriculum.

          1.1-Introduction                                                          
                                                                                    
          The rising need to access academic support in a timely manner has put forth the necessity
          of having an effective communication in university. Traditional academic advising is
          constrained by office hours, the number of personnel available and the increase in students
                                                                                    
          in need of help. Students will usually have difficulties in getting reliable answers to the
          academic questions fast.                                                  
                                                                                    
          This is a process that may take up much time since they have to go through different
          resources such as course catalogs, academic regulations and departmental guidelines. In
          that regard, the given project will attempt to develop a web-based application within the
                                                                                    
          Computer Science Department in Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU).             
                                                                                    
          With this system, the students will have direct access to academic information, by querying
          a structured body of knowledge, consisting of course outlines, academic policies, and
          outlining fee policies. This is aimed at cutting down on administrative burden, enhancing
                                                                                    
          student experience and offering academic guidance to students in a fast and precise way.
          1.2-Problem Definition                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Students in the Computer Science Department at Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU) have a lot of
          trouble getting academic information, especially about acidic matters, this information is so
          scattered across different places like department offices, official university websites, and academic
          advisors, which makes it hard for students to find what they need. Offices are only open for hours,
          so students can’t always get help when they need it. Advisors are also very busy, so students don’t
          always get answers quickly. Many students end up asking the same questions over and over, which
          makes the staff’s work even harder. On top of all this, academic documents like course catalogs
                                                                                    
          and rules are very long and formal, which makes it difficult for students to find the specific
          information they are looking for.                                         
                                                                                    
          .                                                                         
                                                                                    
          1.3 Proposed System                                                       
                                                                                    
          Our project will act as an academic advisor for Computer Science students at Quaid-i-Azam
          University. Main goal is to provide students with fast and easy access to academic information
          through a simple chat interface. Instead of going to the department office, students can simply ask

          1. Dataset                                                                
                                                                                    
          The dataset for our project will include information like course details such as titles, descriptions,
          and credit hours prerequisite courses, fee structure and payment guidelines, class schedules and
          examination timetables, academic policies and departmental rules, as well as final year project and
                                                                                    
          thesis submission guidelines. The information is stored in a database, allowing the system to
          quickly retrieve relevant details when a student asks a question.         
                                                                                    
          2. Preprocessing                                                          
          These steps include:                                                      
                                                                                    
            •  Tokenization which means Splitting the sentence into individual words
                                                                                    
            •  Text Cleaning which means removing unnecessary symbols and characters
                                                                                    
            •  Stop-word Removal that is eliminating common words that do not affect meaning
                                                                                    
            •  Normalization which converting the query into a standard format      
                                                                                    
          These are the steps that help the system to understand the questions more clearly.
                                                                                    
          3. Pretrained Model                                                       
          The system uses a Multilingual BERT model to interpret user queries. The reason why this model
                                                                                    
          is selected is because it is able to comprehend context in sentences, and also it is able to
          accommodate several languages such as English and Urdu. The fact that a pretrained model is used
          also minimizes the time required to train a complex model because still good results are obtained.
                                                                                    
          4. Intent Detection and Information Retrieval                             
                                                                                    
          Following are the steps system followed                                   
                                                                                    
            •  Detects the purpose of the query for example course prerequisites or fee information
                                                                                    
            •  Check terms such as course names                                     
            •  Search the database for matching records                             
                                                                                    
            •  Convert the retrieved information into easy and understandable way for the student

          1.5 Objectives                                                            
                                                                                    
          User Objectives                                                           
                                                                                    
          Our system will help students by providing instant answers to their academic questions. It will
          assist them in understanding course prerequisites, credit hours, fee structure, and academic
          policies in both English and Urdu. This will make it easier for students to get the information
          they need and reduce confusion about academic procedures and semester planning.
                                                                                    
          Technical Objectives                                                      
          A Multilingual BERT trained model will be used in the system to comprehend and answer
          student queries. It will also be structured in terms of knowledge base details of the courses and
          policies. The chatbot will be implemented as a web platform and will be affordable to all
          devices.                                                                  
                                                                                    
          Learning Objectives                                                       
          This system aims to assist students in learning to process natural language queries and
          comprehending the working of NLP models such as BERT, as well as, give them a practical aid
          in academic support.                                                      
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          1.6 Motivation                                                            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            •  Students may frequently be interested in courses, prerequisites and academic policies.
            •  Sometimes the advisors in the departments and academic university may not be present
               and accessible.                                                      
            •  Sometimes there is wasting of time by students in searching the notices, regulations and
               course outlines.                                                     
            •  Because of this, a number of students pose same academic questions to the academic
               again and again; this overburdens the administrative members of staff.
            •  Chatbot can be an AI-based service representing a fast and trustworthy response to
               frequent academic questions.                                         
            •  Intelligent assistants are being exploited in the educational systems, and these types of
               solutions are becoming helpful and pertinent.

          2.1 Introduction                                                          
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          The software requirements for our project are covered in this chapter.Questions regarding
          prerequisites, credit hours, fee structures, class schedules, exam schedules, and thesis
          guidelines, Our system offers students immediate academic support.        
                                                                                    
          Our system can comprehend and answer questions in both Urdu and English thanks to artificial
          intelligence (AI) and natural language processing (NLP),because of which it became possible
          for making our system and reducing the load of academic advisor.          
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          2.2 Product Overview                                                      
                                                                                    
          The AI-Powered Chat-Based Academic Advisor is designed to serve the stakeholders such as:
                                                                                    
          Students:                                                                 
          Who need guidance in courses, prerequisites, fee structure, timetables, thesis requirements, and
                                                                                    
          departmental policies.                                                    
                                                                                    
          Newly Admitted Students:                                                  
          Students who are unfamiliar with departmental procedures or want to take admission in Qau can
          have access to academic information.                                      
                                                                                    
          Academic Advisors:                                                        
          cannot replace academic advisor but will be very helpful in reducing the workload on academic
          advisor or staff members.                                                 
                                                                                    
          Departmental Administration:                                              
          staff can use the system to streamline academic communication and keep information
          organized.

          3.1 Introduction                                                          
                                                                                    
          This chapter presents the design of the proposed system and explains how the system is structured
          to satisfy the requirements identified in the previous chapter. It describes the overall architecture,
          major software components, their interactions, and the design decisions used during development
          of the our project.                                                       
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          The main purpose of the software design chapter is to show that how the component of our
          system are working together so that maintaining the system will become easier and in future our
          system is easy to upgrade or extend.                                      
                                                                                    
          Design Overview                                                           
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
            •  Academic Query Input: Students can ask questions about courses, prerequisites, credit
               hours, fees, and policies.                                           
            •  Courses Information Search: Students can search courses details by name.
            •  Bilingual Support: Our project will understands both English and Urdu queries.
            •  Knowledge Base Retrieval: Our system will fetch the required data from database.
            •  Conversational Responses: Our system provides easily understandable answers in a chat
               format.                                                              
            •  Student Query History: Students can check past queries and responses.
            •  Admin Panel: Admins can update courses, policies, schedules, and other academic info.
                                                                                    
          How Components Interact                                                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Our system will have many core components to ensure better functionality. 
          First of all the chat interface will allow students to ask questions and receive responses.
          Then at the backend API will process the requests sent by the interface, while the NLP
          engine will understand the queries and identify the intent of queries, and take out the key
          information such as course names or fee related terms. Then the database which stores
          structured academic data, including course details, prerequisites, schedules, and policies,
          for quick retrieval. Then the response generator will transform the data into clear and easy
                                                                                    
          answers. In the last , there is an admin panel which allows admins to manage, update data,
          and maintain the system.

          3.2 System Architecture Design                                            
                                                                                    
          This part will provide an overview of how the parts of the software are structured. It explains
          how the front end, backend, NLP layer, and data storage are working together, and how data
          flows between them.                                                       
          The architecture of our system is designed in such a way it is easy to maintain and easy to
          update or upgrade in future. It ensures that our system can process natural language queries,
                                                                                    
          provide academic information, and provide real time responses and making sure that different
          parts of the system communicate in such a way they are effective and that each team member
          has a clear role.                                                         
                                                                                    
          3.2.1 Chosen System Architecture                                          
                                                                                    
          The proposed system is designed using a 4-layer architecture:             
                                                                                    
            1. Presentation Layer                                                   
            2. Application Layer                                                    
            3. NLP Processing Layer                                                 
            4. Data Layer                                                           
                                                                                    
          1. Presentation Layer                                                     
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Overview                                                                  
                                                                                    
          The Presentation Layer is the user view of the system .It allows students and admins to interact
          with the ai advisor through a web-based interface.                        
                                                                                    
          Functionality                                                             
                                                                                    
          • Students can register and log in.                                       
          • Students can ask academic questions in English or Urdu.                 
          • Students can view chatbot responses and query history.                  
          • Admins can log in to the admin panel and update academic records.       
                                                                                    
          Technologies Used

          • HTML, CSS, JavaScript or React                                          
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          2. Application Layer                                                      
                                                                                    
          Overview                                                                  
                                                                                    
          The system's business logic is contained in the application layer. It handles front-end requests and
          arranges communication with the database and NLP module.                  
                                                                                    
          Functionality                                                             
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          • Responds to requests for login and registration                         
          • Responds to students' academic inquiries

          4.1 Introduction                                                          
                                                                                    
          In this chapter, one can learn about the testing plan of the AI-Powered Chat-Based Academic
                                                                                    
          Advisor of the Computer Science Department at Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU). Testing is
          intended to ensure that the system is functioning as per the needs that were identified in the
          previous chapters and has the ability to offer quality academic assistance to the students. Because
          the project is founded on a chatbot, which will answer academic questions, it is not only essential
                                                                                    
          to test the basic functions of a software (e.g., the ability to log in and register), but also to test the
          query processing, information search, bilingual input processing, and administration functions.
                                                                                    
          The process of testing is supposed to verify that the system gives appropriate answers, the invalid
          inputs are handled and the system is able to continue to be used by students, as well as,
          administrators.                                                           
                                                                                    
          4.1.1 System Overview                                                     
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          The AI-Powered Chat-Based Academic Advisor is an academic support web-based platform
          created in the Computer Science Department of Quaid-i-Azam University. The students can
          communicate with a chatbot and see the information concerning the courses, prerequisites, fee
          structure, class schedules, academic regulations, and thesis structures.  
                                                                                    
          The system employs a Multilingual BERT-based NLP model to comprehend student queries
          both in English and Urdu. It then sources the sought information in an organized academic
          knowledge base and yields a response in form of conversations. The system also has a student
          facing feature as well as an administrative panel where academic data is updated, query logs are
          viewed and reports are generated..                                        
                                                                                    
          4.1.2 Test Approach                                                       
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          Testing basically focuses on verifying functional behavior of all the major use cases of the system.
          Both functional testing and selected non-functional testing are considered. The main goal is to
          ensure that our sytem responds correctly, handles invalid input safely, and performs reliably under
          normal usage conditions.                                                  
                                                                                    
          The testing process is primarily based on manual execution of test cases,

          5.1 Conclusion                                                            
                                                                                    
          This document has the implementation of the design and development of My FYP i.e. AI-
          Powered Chat-Based Academic Advisor in the Computer Science Department at Quaid-i-
                                                                                    
          Azam University (QAU). The main objective for this project is to give the student an accessible
          and user friendly experience without depending on the manual system or communication.
                                                                                    
          The source of information involved a structured academic body of knowledge, whereas the NLP
          method to understand and process natural language queries was a Multilingual BERT-based
          approach. All through the project, emphasis was laid on the system design, requirement analysis,
          software architecture, use case modeling, sequence diagrams, class design, and testing. The system
          was more comprehensive as the student-side and the admin-side modules were included in it, as it
          does not answer the questions only, but also lets the academic records be managed and updated by
          the means of an administration panel.                                     
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
          The testing procedures was to check the key scenarios of the system such as student registration
          ,login ,admin login search courses academic queries e.t.c.These tests were conducted to ensure
          that the proposed system can provide accurate answers and be effectively tested by its users.It
          provides an effective way of enhancing communication between the academic department and it
